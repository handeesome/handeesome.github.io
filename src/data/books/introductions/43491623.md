In Zadig, a work of satire, Voltaire makes his forceful arguments against religious and metaphysical orthodoxy. The language is intermediate to advanced level, often closer to intermediate. Voltaire was a French writer, historian and philosopher. His intelligence, wit and unique style made him one of France’s greatest writers and philosophers and a leading figure of French Enlightenment. He advocated fiercely for civil liberties, including freedom of religion, freedom of expression, free trade and separation of church and state.
